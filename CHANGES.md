# Portfolio v5

## Visual swaps
**Netflix project** now renders a streaming catalogue browse grid: three rows
of poster tiles drifting at different speeds, with a scanning beam sweeping
across and tiles flaring plus showing a rating bar as it passes. Original
artwork. I deliberately did not recreate Netflix's logo or its intro
sequence, since both are trademarked brand assets and putting them on your
portfolio would be someone else's IP on your site.

**RailFlow project** is now a railway track in perspective: rails converging
to a vanishing point, sleepers streaming toward the viewer with spacing
compressed near the horizon, a train marker running the line with a
headlight glow, and a trackside signal cycling green, amber, red.

## Video removed
`HeroVideo.tsx`, `VIDEO-GUIDE.md` and `public/hero/` are gone. The hero is
the WebGL variance surface only.

## Dashes removed
Every em dash and en dash is gone from visible copy, and hyphenated compound
words in prose were rewritten rather than just stripped, so nothing reads as
if a character was deleted. Date ranges now read "Jan 2025 to Present".
CSS class names, imports and package names were left untouched, and the
metric arrows are intact.

## NEW: Command palette
Cmd+K or Ctrl+K anywhere, or press slash. Jumps to any section, any of the
ten projects, or fires an action: download résumé, email, LinkedIn, GitHub,
switch theme, print. A small pill in the bottom left advertises it so people
discover it. Most portfolios make you scroll; this one is fully operable
from the keyboard.

## NEW: Print stylesheet
Cmd+P, or the print action in the palette, collapses the whole cinematic
site into a clean one page brief: strips the nav, canvases, atmosphere,
ticker and the Lab, forces light ink on white, and appends full URLs after
links so a printed copy stays useful.
